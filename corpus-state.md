## Why not dump the data?

- **SQLite cache** can be hundreds of MB with FTS + embeddings; regenerable via `index rebuild` / `embeddings import-cache`.
- **Supabase vectors** are a serving layer; the useful public artifact is *freshness, coverage, and hash alignment*, not the float arrays.
- Future uses (drift alerts, multi-node sync dashboards) still only need these **state** fields plus optional targeted samples.
