# Public Twin assets

## JHR signature / paraphe

`jhr-signature.svg` is the public graphical facsimile of Jean Hugues Noël Robert's handwritten signature/paraphe, constructed around the initials **J H R**.

### Semantics and authority

The asset is a **representation of a signature**, not an authentication secret and not, by itself, proof that Jean Hugues Robert signed or approved a particular act.

Keep four notions distinct:

1. **signature representation** - the reusable graphical facsimile;
2. **act of signing** - apposition of that representation, or another signature mechanism, to a specific act;
3. **authority to sign** - an explicit mandate authorizing the act for the relevant document and capacity;
4. **evidence of signing** - the trace/provenance that establishes who authorized and performed the act, when, on which immutable document/version, and under which mandate.

An agent may prepare a document containing this facsimile only when the current mandate explicitly authorizes signature/apposition for the specific act or class of acts. Mere access to this public asset **never grants signing authority**.

Whenever practicable, a signed artifact should retain or reference the mandate, document hash/version, timestamp, acting capacity and delivery trace. Stronger cryptographic/eIDAS mechanisms may be used when the legal or operational stakes require them.

Public availability is intentional: reproducibility of the graphical mark must not be confused with authority or identity authentication.
